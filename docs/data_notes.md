## Data quirks found

- Priority labels carry a ' priority' suffix ('Extreme priority'). Labels do not sort strictly by MR across pilots (e.g. a High at MR 12.0 and an Extreme at MR 11.5); we use the label as given.
- Ranverso has no Catastrophic scenarios, Monti Lucretili has no Low or Medium ones and Baltanas has only 7 scenarios, so not every level exists at every monument.
- Ranverso repeats two descriptions under different keys: 'freeze-thaw cycles' (physical forces, Low; incorrect temperature, Medium) and 'biohazard' (physical forces; pests).
- Ranverso: 26 installed-instrument records but 20 distinct instruments. Humidity x3, temperature x2 and vibration x1 are registered twice under two naming styles. One of the 20 is a virtual Earth Observation layer.
- Ranverso weather station 2CF7F1C0734001E8 is marked verification_required, with temporary coordinates, after being moved from the Baltanas asset state.
- AD-RT rule names are per pilot (e.g. R4b_soil_low at Ranverso, R4b_soil_range at Baltanas), so the mapping is keyed by pilot and rule.
- Policy 'AD-RT Lucretili policy - GNSS z-score' has rule R10_solar_zscore: name and description disagree.
- Many alarm events are exact duplicates (same rule, timestamp and value under different ids); some duplicates carry slightly different values (re-runs).
- All Ranverso indoor-humidity alarms (R1_hum_inside, sensor 168442 at the left altar) have values from 110 to 558 against a threshold of 80, so they are unusable.
- Ranverso DL alarms all attribute 100% to NO2 (sensor 359111) with scores in the millions against a threshold of 142; we do not use them.
- Ranverso soil-moisture alarms stay between 2.457 and 2.476 for three weeks (possible stuck sensor); the first one (28 Aug) has value 0.0 with threshold 0.5 while the current policy threshold is 5.
- Delos and Baltanas alarms in the sample are all deep-learning (DL) alarms without rule names, so the rule-to-sensor check cannot be used there yet.
- Units are not part of the alarm data.
- Ranverso scenario 'evidence' lists are empty ('left empty until AD-RT/EOP observability is configured'), so there is no official link between Ranverso scenarios and sensors or rules.

## Assumptions needing human review

Game-design rules (in code):

- Levels = ABC priority labels. A level is offered only if the monument has at least one scenario with that label. Missions have 3 threats; when a level has fewer, we borrow from the next lower level, then higher.
- Alarm plausibility: humidity and soil moisture must be 0 to 100, rain 0 to 1000, temperature -50 to 60. An exact 0 for humidity or soil moisture is treated as a sensor dropout.
- Alarm episodes: alarms of one rule less than 48 hours apart form one episode; the game features the first, the strongest (largest multiple of the threshold) and the last alarm of each episode, at most 10.
- Score: 25 points per mission threat covered by at least one capable card (75) plus up to 25 for budget efficiency (share of coins spent on cards that cover a mission threat or the replayed alarm). The historical alarm is shown but not scored.
- Detection: the player's network detects an alarm if, for every group in the rule's detect_with, it has at least one card from that group. Real sensor placement (inside or outside, which wall) is ignored. A level without a usable alarm (e.g. DL-only sites) skips the replay with a short message.
- Reveal counts distinct ARGUS instruments (not installed-instrument records) and groups them by card.
